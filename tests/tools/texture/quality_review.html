Rendered images sample the complete chain using the production anisotropic surface sampler. Downsizing the render target drives implicit LOD; cubemap derivatives and filtering can blend neighbouring levels. Normal views use the shared normal decoder and a fixed diffuse light. Colour and lit normals are encoded for sRGB display; packed channels remain numeric. These are texture review renders, not captures of a full PBR scene.
